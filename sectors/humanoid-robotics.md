# Sector: Humanoid Robotics

- **Language:** English
- **Goal:** keep the reader on top of the humanoid robotics industry *and* research, from
  money and markets to the technical frontier.

## What to cover

1. **Funding and finance:** rounds, valuations, lead investors, IPO plans, public-company
   moves (Tesla, Nvidia, Hyundai, UBTech, XPeng, Xiaomi…), M&A, sovereign or state funds.
2. **Products and launches:** new robots, new versions, pricing, specs (DoF, payload,
   runtime, hands), availability.
3. **Deployments and scale:** factory or warehouse pilots versus real paid deployments,
   production volumes, new factories and manufacturing plans, customer contracts.
4. **AI for robots:** robot foundation models and VLAs (vision-language-action), world
   models, simulation and synthetic data, teleoperation and data collection, open-source
   releases.
5. **Research:** notable papers (arXiv cs.RO / cs.LG, CoRL, RSS, ICRA, IROS, Humanoids,
   NeurIPS, ICLR), especially **reinforcement learning, sim-to-real, whole-body control,
   locomotion, dexterous manipulation, loco-manipulation**. Mention the lab and the key
   result in one technical sentence.
6. **Hardware and supply chain:** actuators, reducers, dexterous hands, tactile sensing,
   batteries, compute (e.g. Nvidia Jetson Thor), component suppliers and costs.
7. **Regulation and politics:** safety standards (ISO), EU AI Act and Machinery Regulation,
   US and China industrial policy, export controls, subsidies, labour debates.
8. **Talent and ecosystem:** key hires and departures, new startups, accelerators,
   competitions, notable hiring pushes (useful for the reader's career).

## Sources

Prefer primary sources and quality reporting:

- **Primary:** company blogs and press releases, arXiv, project pages, official filings,
  government announcements.
- **Industry / tech press:** The Robot Report, IEEE Spectrum (Robotics), TechCrunch,
  The Information, The Verge, Wired, MIT Technology Review, Humanoids Daily.
- **Financial press:** Reuters, Bloomberg, Financial Times, Wall Street Journal, CNBC,
  Business Insider.
- **China coverage:** South China Morning Post, Caixin Global, Reuters, Bloomberg,
  CnEVPost, TechNode, Pandaily.

Avoid content farms, AI-generated aggregators and pure hype videos. A viral demo is only
news if it shows a real capability jump; say what is new.

## Analysis angle

For each main story, Newton's take should answer at least one of:
- Who gains or loses competitively (US vs. China vs. Europe, incumbents vs. startups)?
- Is this real traction (paid deployment, volume, revenue) or a demo / announcement?
- What does it mean for valuations, for the funding climate, or for a founder entering now?
- What is the technical significance (method, data, compute, hardware bottleneck)?

## Weekly landscape (landscape day only)

A short state of play in 4–6 sentences: total funding announced this week and the biggest
round, who shipped or deployed, the most important research result, the trend of the week,
and one opportunity or open question worth watching.
